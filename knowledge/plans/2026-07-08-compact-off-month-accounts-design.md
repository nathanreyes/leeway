# Compact Off-Month Accounts Design

## Change

Past and future month views should keep the Accounts panel visible, but smaller.
It should explain the rule without taking much vertical space from the budget
panels.

## Accounts Panel

Use a four-row panel and two lines of copy:

```text
Account balances apply only to the current month.
This month uses plan snapshot math: income - bills - envelopes.
```

## Summary Panel

Move the off-month account-balance note to the top of Summary, where the
current-month account-derived terms normally appear:

```text
account balances count only in the current month
+ $10,000.00 income left  - $4,318.00 bills left
+     $0.00 envelopes
=  $5,682.00 what's left
```

This keeps the rule close to the missing funds/card/carry terms while avoiding
duplicate explanatory text.
