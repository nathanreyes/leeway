# What's left

Leeway answers one question: after the bills you still owe and the money you still plan to spend, what's left? It's a forecast, not a ledger — you don't record every purchase.

## The pieces

Accounts hold your real balance today. Income and Expenses are dated money you toggle as it settles. Envelopes are spending you expect but haven't itemized. The summary rolls them all into a single what's-left number.

## A month at a glance

```
  Checking              2,400
  + income to come      1,800
  - bills to pay         -950
  - envelopes left       -430
  ---------------------------
  what's left           2,820
```

## Getting around

- `Tab` — move between panels
- `h` — help for the focused panel
- `P` — plans (reusable templates)
- `S` — selected series, then all series
- `q` — quit
