# Plans

Plans are simply a collection of income, bills, and envelopes. They are the baseline, ready to stamp into any month you choose, present or future.

Typically, you'll create a plan for your baseline month and then stamp it into each new month.

But you can also use plans to compare scenarios, like taking a vacation or buying a new car, without affecting your baseline plan. Stamp future months with different plans to see how your budget changes.

## How it fits

Stamp a plan into a month to create an independent snapshot you can then adjust freely. Editing a plan afterwards doesn't touch months you've already stamped — a plan is the starting point, not a live link.

Furthermore, editing a month's bills or envelopes doesn't change the plan either. You can use a plan to create a month, then tweak that month without affecting the plan.

## Example

```
  Plan: Monthly baseline
    Income      3,000
    Rent        1,500
    Internet       80
    Groceries     600 · automatic
    Dining        200 · manual

  Stamp -> the month opens with these bills and envelopes.
```

## Keys

This list is the master pane; the selected plan's Income, Expenses, and Envelopes fill the panes to the right. Tab moves focus between them; these keys act on the plan while the list is focused.

- `j` / `k` — move between plans
- `n` — create a new plan
- `l` — rename the selected plan
- `s` — stamp the selected plan onto a month
- `x` — delete the selected plan (stamped months are kept)
- `Tab` — move focus into the item panes
