# Plan · Envelopes

The spending buckets a plan sets up — how much to budget for groceries, dining, and the like in a typical month.

## How it fits

Stamping copies these envelopes into the month, where they draw down over time. How an envelope behaves — automatic vs manual, its cadence — lives on the underlying series and is edited on the Series page (S).

## Example

```
  Plan: Monthly baseline
    Groceries   600   automatic
    Dining      200   manual

  Stamp -> the month gets fresh envelopes.
```
