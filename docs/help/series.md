# Series

Series are the durable identities behind recurring items — the 'Rent' or 'Groceries' that persists across months and plans even as labels or amounts change.

## How it fits

When you reuse an item in a plan or month, it points at a series. That's how Leeway links the same bill across time for trends, and why envelope behavior (automatic/manual, cadence) is edited here once and respected everywhere the series is used.

## Example

```
  Groceries (envelope · automatic)
    used by:  Monthly baseline, Lean month
    seen in:  May, Jun, Jul

  Rename here -> every month follows.
```
