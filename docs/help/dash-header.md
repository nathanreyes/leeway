# Months

The Months section sets which month the budget workspace shows. Leeway works one month at a time: every total in the detail area is for this period.

## How it fits

Move through stamped months to review old budgets or plan ahead. A month you reach with `g` can appear before it has been stamped. Choose a plan below it and press `s` to fill it in.

## Save a month as a what-if

Trimmed a month by hand and want to budget that way again? Press `w` on a stamped month and give it a name. Leeway saves the month as a what-if on Main: each amount you changed, item you removed, and item you added from a series becomes one of its changes. Items you typed into the month as one-offs stay out. The what-if keeps following Main's later edits, and the month now records it as its plan, so restamping uses it. Press `A` on the what-if to write its changes into Main.

## Keys

- `j` / `k` — move through months and plans
- `g` — go to any month
- `w` — save the month as a what-if on Main
- `tab` — move into the selected month's panels
