# Expenses

Expenses are the dated bills you owe this month — rent, utilities, subscriptions, loan payments.

## How it fits

A bill counts against what's left until you mark it paid. Paid bills have already left your balance, so they stop being subtracted. The forecast always assumes an unpaid bill will be paid, so you never overcommit money you owe.

## Example

```
  Rent           1,500   paid
  Electric          90   to pay

  'To pay' reduces what's left now;
  marking it paid clears it from the math.
```
